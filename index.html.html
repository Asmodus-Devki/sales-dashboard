SQL — Data Extraction Query
SELECT product_name,
  SUM(revenue) AS total_revenue,
  COUNT(order_id) AS units_sold
FROM sales_fact_2024
GROUP BY product_name
ORDER BY total_revenue DESC
LIMIT 5;
Key Insights & Strategic Recommendations
Analyst Findings — FY 2024
Exploratory data analysis performed using Python + Pandas · Visualized via Power BI & Chart.js
EDA Summary
Revenue Growth
Q4 surged +28.4% vs Q3, driven by holiday campaigns and bundled electronics promotions. December alone accounted for 10.9% of annual revenue.
Regional Opportunity
The West region contributes only 16% of revenue despite comparable population density. A targeted digital campaign could unlock an estimated $120K–$150K in additional revenue.
Top Segment
Electronics dominates with 28.3% revenue share and the highest average order value at $416. Recommended for increased inventory investment in Q1 2025.
Customer Retention
Repeat customers represent 62% of total orders, with an average of 2.8 purchases per customer. Loyalty programme expansion could raise this to 3.5+ purchases.
Seasonality Pattern
A recurring July dip (−7.7% vs June) appears in both 2023 and 2024 data, suggesting a seasonal slowdown. A mid-summer promotion strategy is recommended to flatten the curve.
AOV Optimisation
Average order value grew modestly at +4.7%. Upsell and cross-sell recommendations (e.g., accessories with electronics) could push AOV above $300 by mid-2025.
